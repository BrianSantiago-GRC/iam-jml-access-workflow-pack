# JML Workflow

Date: 2026-05-16  
Purpose: Define a repeatable identity lifecycle process for Joiner, Mover, and Leaver events.

## 1) Joiner (New Hire)

1. HR confirms hire date, department, manager, and role.
2. IT/IAM creates account with standard role-based baseline access.
3. MFA setup is required at first sign-in.
4. Any non-standard access requires manager approval.
5. Access completion is documented in ticket notes.

## 2) Mover (Role Change)

1. HR or manager submits role change request with effective date.
2. Current access is reviewed against new role requirements.
3. Remove old role access that is no longer needed.
4. Add new role access after manager/system-owner approval.
5. Confirm least-privilege alignment and document changes.

## 3) Leaver (Offboarding)

1. HR submits termination or separation notice with effective date/time.
2. Disable account access at required time.
3. Remove group memberships and privileged roles.
4. Revoke VPN, email, app, and shared drive access.
5. Transfer ownership for required files/mailbox items.
6. Record completion evidence and closure in offboarding ticket.

## 4) SLA Targets (Sample)

- Joiner standard access: same business day or before start day
- Mover access update: within 1 business day of approved request
- Leaver account disablement: immediately at separation time

## 5) Evidence to Keep

- Access request ticket
- Approval trail
- Before/after role membership evidence
- Offboarding completion checklist
